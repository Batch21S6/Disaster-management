DISASTER AWARENESS

Know the Threats

Understanding disasters helps us respond quickly and safely.

🔍
🌎

Earthquake

Sudden shaking of the ground caused by movement of tectonic plates.

🌊

Flood

Overflow of water that covers normally dry areas.

🌀

Cyclone

A powerful storm with strong winds and heavy rainfall.

🔥

Fire

Uncontrolled burning that can threaten people and property.

⛰️

Landslide

Movement of soil, rocks and debris down a slope.

🌊

Tsunami

Powerful ocean waves caused by underwater disturbances.

☀️

Drought

A long period with unusually low rainfall and water shortage.

⛈️

Thunderstorm

Storm involving lightning, thunder, rain and strong winds.

SAFETY GUIDELINES

What To Do During a Disaster

01

Make an Emergency Plan

Discuss evacuation routes and meeting points with your family.

02

Prepare Emergency Supplies

Keep food, water, medicines, batteries and other essential items ready.

03

Stay Informed

Follow official warnings and emergency announcements.

🛡️
01

Stay Calm

Remain calm and avoid panic so you can make safe decisions.

02

Move to a Safe Place

Follow evacuation instructions and move away from dangerous areas.

03

Help Others

Assist children, elderly people and others when it is safe.

🚨
01

Check for Injuries

Check yourself and others and seek medical assistance when necessary.

02

Avoid Damaged Areas

Stay away from damaged buildings, wires and unsafe roads.

03

Follow Official Updates

Return only when authorities confirm that the area is safe.
